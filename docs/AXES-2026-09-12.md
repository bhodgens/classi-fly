# Axes (2026-09-12) — the four untested levers, and the one that matters

Companion to `docs/EXPERIMENTS.md` (same protocol throughout: 5-fold seed 42,
n=361 gold cases, 1 case = 0.28 pt, route counts disclosed). Baseline to beat:
reservoir A=0.7258, gated 71/361, P 0.958, C 0.197, E2E 0.8857.

Runners and raw results:
`tools/eval/axis_spectral_projection.{py,json}`,
`axis_mb_subcircuit.{py,json}`, `axis_readout_heads.{py,json}`,
`axis_robustness.{py,json}`.

## Axis A — spectral radius + trained input projection: PARITY

- **Spectral radius.** Swept rho in {0.5, 0.8, 0.95, 1.0, 1.1, 1.3}. rho=0.5
  ties the shipped `weights/127` scaling exactly (A 0.7258); every larger rho
  degrades (0.721 down to 0.709). The default scaling is already in the best
  regime.
- **Trained input projection** (BPTT on W_in, gradient verified by finite
  differences — which caught and fixed a real transpose bug). 14 configs, mean
  0.7179, at or below the fixed-projection 0.7258. The best config (0.7285,
  +1 case) is a spike, not a trend.
- **Combined** (best rho x trained projection) is the worst row (0.7064).
- Diagnostic worth keeping: the effective transition `0.8*I + W` has spectral
  radius 1.17 with 19 eigenvalues outside the unit circle — not a strict echo
  state reservoir — yet tanh bounds it (saturation 0.0%) and it performs best.
  Echo-state-property orthodoxy is not what does the work here.

## Axis B — mushroom body subcircuit: WORSE

Identification was annotation-driven, not approximated: Winding 2023
supplemental table S2 `celltype` joined to the adjacency rows (2,606 of 2,952
rows labelled, zero ID conflicts). MB = KC+MBON+MBIN+MB-FBN+MB-FFN = 382
neurons, 5,613 edges.

| representation | n | A | routes/361 | P | E2E |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| whole brain | 2,952 | 0.7318 | 68 | 0.956 | 0.8920 |
| MB full | 382 | 0.6908 | 31 | 0.936 | 0.8787 |
| MB core (KC+MBON+MBIN) | 220 | 0.7014 | 71 | 0.916 | 0.8922 |
| KC only | 144 | 0.6359 | 54 | 0.926 | 0.8865 |
| random size-matched 382 (mean of 5) | 382 | 0.7105 | 58 | 0.922 | 0.8877 |
| degree-hub top-382 (density control) | 382 | 0.7029 | 85 | 0.871 | 0.8959 |

MB alone is 4.1 pt / 15 cases WORSE than the whole brain on accuracy, and sits
below the mean of a random subgraph of the same size. The density confound was
controlled (MB is 5.5x denser than random; the densest same-size control also
fails to beat the whole brain). **The learning center is not the special part.**

## Axis C — readout heads, and the ridge-lambda defect fixed

| head (larval states) | routes | P | C | A_all | E2E | OOD-R |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ridge lam=1e-3 (old default) | 50 | 0.900 | 0.139 | 0.634 | 0.8724 | 0.929 |
| **ridge lam=30** | 71 | **0.958** | 0.197 | **0.726** | **0.8857** | 0.929 |
| ridge auto inner-CV (the fix) | 78 | 0.949 | 0.216 | 0.718 | 0.8854 | 0.929 |
| centroid + cosine margin (campaign champion) | 22 | 0.955 | 0.061 | 0.693 | 0.8733 | 0.893 |
| kNN-5 unanimity | 67 | 0.851 | 0.186 | 0.540 | 0.8648 | 0.643 |
| logistic (L2 by inner CV) | 101 | 0.911 | 0.280 | 0.720 | 0.8800 | 0.786 |

No head beats ridge+margin on E2E. The logistic head routes 2.8x more cases at
lower precision (P 0.911 vs 0.958) — a coverage/precision trade, not a win. The
campaign's centroid+cosine-margin champion does not transfer to reservoir
states (coverage collapses to 0.061).

**Defect fixed** in `tools/train/train_readout.py`: lambda is now resolved per
problem instead of hardcoded — the legacy 1e-3 is kept byte-identically for
narrow readouts (features < WIDE_READOUT_DIM=64), otherwise a deterministic
stratified inner CV over a 1e-3..1000 grid, with a features/samples heuristic
fallback for very few rows and a `--ridge-lambda` override. On the reference
problem it selects 30.0, the measured optimum (per fold: 30, 30, 10, 30, 30).
Six new tests pin the policy; `python3 -m pytest tools/train tools/verify -q`
gives 44 passed.

## Axis D — robustness under corrupted embeddings: THE ONE REAL WIN

Corruptions applied to TEST folds only, identical corrupted tensors for every
representation, readouts trained clean.

| condition | probe | larval | synthetic | larval - probe |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| clean | 259 | 262 | 264 | +3 |
| dropout 10% | 262 | 256 | 253 | -6 |
| dropout 25% | 250 | 249 | 233 | -1 |
| dropout 50% | 197 | 225 | 225 | **+28** (p=0.002) |
| truncate last 50% | 224 | 248 | 240 | **+24** (p=0.006) |
| truncate last 75% | 140 | 208 | 204 | **+68** (p<1e-12) |
| noise sigma=0.1 | 127 | 129 | 101 | +2 |
| noise sigma=0.2 | 91 | 86 | 62 | -5 |

Independently reproduced by the orchestrator (own code, own folds): +24
(p=0.0063) at 50% truncation, +68 (p=2.6e-12) at 75% truncation, +28 (p=0.0018)
at 50% dropout; parity under dense Gaussian noise. Direction and significance
are solid.

**Mechanism decomposition** (orchestrator run) — the win is NOT the connectome:

| representation | clean | trunc 50% | trunc 75% | dropout 50% |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| probe on embeddings | 259 | 224 | 140 | 197 |
| random projection only, no recurrence | 224 | 216 | 186 | 195 |
| larval reservoir, 4 steps | 262 | 248 | 208 | 225 |
| larval reservoir, 8 steps | 261 | 235 | 166 | 207 |
| synthetic reservoir, 4 steps | 264 | 240 | 178 | 225 |

Reading: the **random projection alone** recovers most of the robustness
(trunc-75: 186 versus the probe's 140), and the **recurrence adds more** at 4
steps (208 versus 186). But **8 steps is worse than 4** (166 / 207), and larval
versus synthetic at 4 steps is parity except for one condition (+30 at
trunc-75, which does not reproduce at 8 steps). A fixed sparse random matrix
plus about four tanh steps buys graceful degradation; the specific fly wiring
does not.

## Consolidated verdict

| axis | verdict |
|---|---|
| spectral radius scaling | PARITY — default scaling already optimal |
| trained input projection | PARITY to WORSE |
| mushroom-body subcircuit | WORSE (below a random same-size subgraph) |
| readout head variants | no head beats ridge+margin (ridge-lambda default fixed) |
| clean accuracy versus a linear probe | PARITY (within 1-3 cases) |
| **robustness to missing input dimensions** | **RESERVOIR WINS: +19 pt at 75% truncation, +7.8 pt at 50% dropout** |

The reservoir's value is not accuracy. It is **graceful degradation when input
dimensions go missing** — and that property is available from a synthetic
(license-free) matrix, so the connectome is not required for it either.
